# Value proposition

Bet on sport like on SNAI or Betfair. Pick your games, see one total, stake in euros, get paid.

- **Pay with any asset.** USDC, SOL or BONK. The swap happens inside the bet.
- **Frictionless experience.** No trading jargon: euros, odds and payouts on screen, never
  tokens or positions. It feels like any web2 betting app: pick, stake, bet.
- **Sponsored transactions (no SOL needed).** Our sponsor key pays the network fees and rent:
  every transaction is multi-signed, you sign your bet and the sponsor co-signs as fee payer.
  No wallet needed either: continue with Google through Privy and get one made for you. Someone
  who has never touched crypto can sign up and place a first bet.
- **The bookmaker hedges parlays on Pascal.** Every price comes from Pascal's live order books,
  and every parlay we take is hedged leg by leg on Pascal. Our margin is shown, not hidden.
  On devnet the hedges are signed for Pascal but mock-filled: test money, no real trades.
- **Cash out mid-game.** While games are still being played, take your money early at a price
  from the live books: one tap, settled on chain.
- **Copy-trade from verified tipsters.** A tipster's record is built only from settled bets they
  staked themselves; verified means at least 5 settled tips and 20 USDC of their own money. Each
  tip shows how much the tipster staked on it and its pool: the total other bettors staked
  copying the same parlay. One tap copies it.

Markets: NBA winner, spread and total; EPL, Bundesliga and LaLiga full-time result (1-X-2).
One to five legs per bet, one leg per game.
