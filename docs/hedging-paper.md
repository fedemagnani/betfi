# Hedging derivation

*Pricing Parlays by Hedging on Prediction-Market Order Books*: the proofs behind
[Math](math.md). It covers the single-event hedge, the static superhedge and the Fréchet bound,
dynamic hedging with and without frictions, the quoted odd as a fixed point, dependent legs,
$n$ legs, and a simulation of two football matches.
